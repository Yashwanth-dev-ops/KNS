## 3. Market context

**Construction cost in Hyderabad, 2026 (published rates):**
- Infralens (updated May 2026): basic about ₹1,720, standard about ₹2,205, premium about ₹2,977 a sft.
- Brick & Bolt: basic ₹1,550, premium ₹2,500 a sft.
- JSW Homes: ₹1,700–2,300; Grihashakti: ₹1,400–2,000.

**KNS's rates are at or below the market** (Standard ₹1,600–1,800 vs about ₹1,720–2,205; Premium ₹1,900–2,200 vs ₹2,500–2,977), with materials included. That is a strong, honest selling point, *if* the spec is comparable. Expect buyers to compare it line by line.

**Kondapur prices:**
- 3BHK apartments: roughly ₹8,000–10,500 a sft in listings (Square Yards ₹10,350; Housing.com ₹8,400–9,100). At those rates a 1,650 sft flat would list at about ₹1.3–1.7 crore. This is context only, not KNS's price.
- Independent houses: listings around ₹3–5 crore. One 4BHK of 3,550 sft is listed at ₹3.73 crore. The data is thin.

**Competitors' websites (the bar buyers compare against).** Brick & Bolt offers:
- a cost calculator by Hyderabad locality, with a package breakdown,
- milestone payments through escrow,
- a published structural warranty (10 or 15 years, depending on which page),
- "470+ quality checks per floor",
- thousands of house plans.

KNS's website has none of the trust items yet: **warranty, payment schedule, quality process, timeline**. The 3D world and the real projects are where KNS can be *better*; those trust facts are where it is currently *behind*.

## 5. Legal: RERA (Telangana)

- Projects **over 500 sq m of land or with more than 8 units** must be registered with TG-RERA **before any marketing, advertising or sale**. That includes plotted developments.
- The Raghavendra Colony site (600 sq yd ≈ 502 sq m, 10 flats) and the Ganapatiguda villa layout both appear to fall under this.
- TG-RERA is actively enforcing it, with fines including ₹2.85 lakh, ₹9.96 lakh, ₹14.9 lakh and ₹29.96 lakh, and stop orders on advertising. In 2026 it warned specifically against misleading pre-launch adverts.
- **Recommendation:** don't publish those two projects' sale pages until their RERA numbers are on them. The individual duplex and the turnkey construction service are not affected. Confirm with a lawyer.
